Face

Face & injectables

Soften lines, restore volume and define your profile.

  • Botox, Dysport & XeominSmooths frown lines, forehead lines and crow's feet. Results show in 5 to 10 days and last 3 to 4 months.
  • Dermal fillers & RadiesseLips, cheeks, jawline, chin, smile lines and under-eyes. Results are immediate and last 6 to 18 months.
  • PDO thread liftDissolvable threads that lift the mid-face, neck and brows, with results lasting up to 18 months.
  • KybellaAn FDA-approved injection that permanently dissolves stubborn fat under the chin.
  • Medical toxRelief for jaw clenching and grinding, excessive sweating and chronic migraines.

Skin & laser

Even tone, smoother texture, firmer skin.

  • Morpheus8Radiofrequency microneedling that remodels the deeper layers of skin on the face and body.
  • Microneedling & PRPCollagen induction for acne scars, pores and fine lines, with your own platelets for added renewal.
  • Lumecca IPLIntense pulsed light for sun spots, freckles, melasma, redness and spider veins.
  • FormaGentle radiofrequency skin tightening, suitable for all skin types.
  • Laser hair removalAny area or the whole body, with built-in cooling for comfort and long-lasting results.
  • Medical-grade facialsThe Ivy Facial, Oxygenating, Skin Brightening, Acne + Oil Control and back facials, plus dermaplaning, peels and microdermabrasion.

Body

Contour, tighten and smooth.

  • T-Shape 2Radiofrequency, laser and vacuum therapy for cellulite, firmer skin and a more defined contour.
  • Forma & Morpheus8 bodyTightening and remodeling for the abdomen, back, thighs, knees, chest, scars and stretch marks.
  • Other spa servicesWaxing, spider vein treatment and nail fungus treatment.

Wellness
