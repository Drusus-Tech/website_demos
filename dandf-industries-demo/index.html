Heads: D&F. It could have been F&D.

A Studebaker, $70 and a coin toss.

Four generations later, the business is still built on what the founders started with: excellence, integrity, cooperation and personal service.

  1. 1953

    H&H Electric opens

    Frank Hausenfluck Sr. and Don Hopper start H&H Electric and Plumbing Company with a mortgaged yellow 1949 Studebaker pickup and a $70 operating budget. They weren’t even paid for their first job.

  2. 1968

    D&F, by a coin flip

    Time to incorporate, but another company already had the H&H name. So the partners used their first initials, Don and Frank, and let a coin decide the order. D&F Industries, Inc. was born. Not F&D.

  3. Today

    Fourth generation

    Still family-owned and still in Pharr, D&F has grown into a major electrical and mechanical contractor for commercial and industrial buildings, high-rises, hospitals and plants across South Texas.
