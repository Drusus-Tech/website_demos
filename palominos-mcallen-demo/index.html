Nuestra historia

Back to our Sonoran roots.

In 1974, Don César Pavlovich Sugich opened the first Palominos — the first restaurant of its kind in the entire state of Sonora — built around family, the grill and traditional carne asada.

His “Paquete Palominos” paid homage to the cooking of the cattle ranchers who settled the region. Half a century and eleven locations later, McAllen is the first table north of the border — and every guest who walks in is part of the family.
