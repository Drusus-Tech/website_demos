What's your AC doing?

Pick what you're seeing. We'll tell you what it usually means, one thing you can check yourself, and what we'd do about it.

Warm air from the vents

Usually means
  • Dirty indoor or outdoor coils
  • Low refrigerant from a leak
  • A worn capacitor or contactor
What we do

AC repair: find the cause, explain your options, and get it cooling again.

Check first: thermostat set to Cool, and a clean filter. A clogged filter alone can choke airflow.

Call for a repair

Water where it shouldn't be

Usually means
  • A clogged condensate drain line
  • A frozen evaporator coil thawing out
  • A cracked or rusted drain pan
What we do

Clear the line, find why it backed up or froze, and protect your ceiling and floors.

Check first: if you see ice on the copper lines, turn the system off and let it thaw before it runs again.

Call about a leak

New noises

Usually means
  • A failing fan or blower motor
  • Loose panels, mounts or parts
  • An electrical part struggling to start
What we do

Diagnose it before a small part takes out a big one like the compressor.

Check first: grinding or a burning smell? Shut it off at the thermostat and call.

Call now

Bills creeping up

Usually means
  • Missed tune-ups and dirty coils
  • Dirty or leaky ductwork
  • Thin attic insulation working against you
What we do

A maintenance visit, duct cleaning, or blown-in Owens Corning® insulation. Insulation evaluations are free.

Good to know: a comfort plan keeps tune-ups on the calendar so you don't have to remember.

Book a check-up

The air feels off

Usually means
  • Dust and debris built up in the ducts
  • Too much humidity for the system to pull out
  • Not enough filtration or fresh air
What we do

Indoor air quality: duct cleaning and sanitation, air filtration, dehumidifiers and ventilation.

Check first: when was the filter last changed? In Valley summers it goes fast.

Ask about air quality
