Logo ideas for Lavish Looks

Your logo is already on your sign, your door and your Instagram, so the demo keeps it. These are three directions side by side: your own logo as the site uses it, plus two new ideas, just for fun.

Your current logo. Metallic gold capitals with the violet V, exactly like the lit sign in the studio. At small sizes the words LOOKS and MED SPA get hard to read on a phone.

Idea 1Your logo, sharpened Used on the demo site

The same letters, traced from your file into a crisp vector so it stays sharp on any screen. The gold finish and violet V are untouched; LOOKS and MED SPA are bigger and bolder so they read on a phone.

Kept: the LAVISH lettering, its spacing, the metallic gold, the violet V and the two lines beside LOOKS.

Changed: LOOKS is about a third bigger and MED SPA is about 70% bigger, both slightly bolder. New favicon from the violet V.

Idea 2The Crown V

“Feel like royalty” becomes a symbol: the violet V from your sign wears a three-point gold crown. The name is set in Melodrama, a dramatic high-contrast serif.

Kept: violet V, metallic gold, the royalty idea from your own voice.

Changed: a new symbol and a new serif wordmark. A logo concept, not your official logo.

Idea 3The LL frame

Two L’s turn around each other inside a square, like the framed acrylic sign on your wall. A gold L and a violet L, with a bold, wide-spaced wordmark.

Kept: your gold and violet, the wide-spaced capitals.

Changed: a new monogram and a sturdier sans wordmark. A logo concept, not your official logo.
