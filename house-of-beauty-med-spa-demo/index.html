Before you come in

  1. Shave the night before

    Every laser area should be clean and freshly shaved so the light reaches the root.

  2. Eat before whitening

    You won’t be able to eat for two hours after laser teeth whitening, and brush gently that day.

  3. Pick your tan timing

    Rapid develops in 1 to 5 hours. Regular takes 8 to 12, perfect overnight.
